# Scope and screen shape

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

What does the dashboard become once the schedule system is dropped, and what is on the screen?

## Answer

- The device is a pure media display. Weather, transit, calendar, routines, medication and the alert banner are removed along with day types, time blocks and special conditions.
- All four media (game, movie, show, book) are always on screen.
- One medium holds the **Spotlight**: a large card featuring exactly one title with its artwork. Each other medium has a small **Medium card** showing its latest activity.
- The Spotlight is re-picked on every device refresh (every 30 minutes), weighted toward fresh activity, never the same title twice in a row.
- A title without artwork is not eligible for the Spotlight, but the system should try to find a poster through a Catalog first.
- Watching several episodes of a show produces one entry for the show, not one per episode.
- A Spotlight has a **Mode**. The modes and their rules are settled in [Modes and Spotlight rotation rules](04-modes-and-spotlight-rotation-rules.md).
- Ratings are labelled by whose they are: the owner's on a Finished title, a friend's on a friend's entry, the public average on a recommendation.
