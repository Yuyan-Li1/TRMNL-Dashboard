# Modes and Spotlight rotation rules

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

What are the Spotlight's modes, when is a title eligible for each, and how is the next Spotlight chosen?

Open points, each already put to the owner with a recommendation:

1. **Friend activity as a fourth mode.** The owner wants friends' entries even for movies already seen, so they are not always recommendations. Proposed: four modes, with Friend activity meaning "what a friend said about a title, whether or not the owner has seen it", and Recommendation meaning a title the owner has not consumed.
2. **Caught up mid-season.** The owner said a show is Finished when the latest available episode has been watched. A weekly show would then be Finished every week. Proposed: Finished only when a season or the show has ended and all of it is watched; a mid-season caught-up show stays In progress with a "caught up" label.
3. **Mode shares.** Proposed: In progress 35%, Finished 25%, Friend activity 20%, Recommendation 20%, fresher activity favoured within a mode, a mode skipped when it has nothing eligible.
4. **Eligibility window.** Proposed: a Finished title or a friend's entry stays eligible for 14 days, matching the 14-day window for games and shows In progress.

Already agreed per medium: games are In progress when played in the last 14 days and never Finished; movies are never In progress and are Finished when logged; books are In progress on the currently-reading list and Finished when marked read.

## Answer

The owner accepted all four proposals on 2026-11-17.

- **Four modes**: In progress, Finished, Friend activity, Recommendation. Friend activity is what a friend said about a title, whether or not the owner has consumed it. Recommendation is always a title the owner has not consumed.
- **Shows**: Finished only when a season or the whole show has ended and the owner has watched all of it. A show whose latest aired episode is watched while the season is still running stays In progress, labelled "caught up".
- **Shares**: In progress 35%, Finished 25%, Friend activity 20%, Recommendation 20%. Fresher activity is favoured within a mode. A mode with nothing eligible is skipped.
- **Eligibility window**: a Finished title or a friend's entry is eligible for 14 days. Games and shows are In progress when played or watched in the last 14 days.

Per medium:

| Medium | In progress | Finished |
|---|---|---|
| Game | Played in the last 14 days | Never |
| Movie | Never | Logged in the diary |
| Show | Episode watched in the last 14 days, including "caught up" | Season or show ended and fully watched |
| Book | On the currently-reading list | Marked as read |
